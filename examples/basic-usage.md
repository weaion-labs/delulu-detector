# Basic Usage Examples

## Standard Mode

**Input:** "He said he's not ready for a relationship but he still texts me every day and likes all my posts. We're basically together right?"

**Output:**
```
🎯 Delulu Rating: 7/10 — Full Delulu
🏷️ Type: Situationship Delulu

🧠 Reality check: Someone who wants to be with you doesn't need you to interpret their behavior.

👉 Next step: Ask him directly: "Are we exclusive?" and see what he says. Then believe him.
```
